# Disk-O Jockey

## Hardware

> **Note:** This technically isn't hardware, it is more of a physical option/accessory. Still, it isn't software, and I plan to do something sofware/hardware based here in the future.

### Optical disk packaging

This project is all about DVDs and CDs, along with other types of optical disks. In the past few months (since 2025, Thursday, February 20th) I have stumbled across a Wikipedia article about Optical Disk Packaging, and I have been fond of the article, as it gives me nostalgia, despite not using this type of packaging very often in my life. I feel it should be replicated in some way with the Disk-O Jockey projects.
